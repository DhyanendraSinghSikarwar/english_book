# Business English Vocabulary Book

**1000+ essential words for Indian and European/American corporate environments**

Part 1: Indian Corporate Environment · Part 2: European/American Corporate Environment

Each part is organized into tiers, moving from the most common everyday terms to advanced, boardroom-level vocabulary.

---

## Part 1: Indian Corporate Environment

### Tier 0 — Everyday Essentials (Most Common)

#### 1. EOD (End of Day)

**Meaning:** By the close of the current working day.

**Synonyms:** *by evening, by day's end, before closing time*

**Examples:**
1. Can you share the revised numbers by EOD today?
2. The finance team needs the approved invoices by EOD Friday.

---

#### 2. COB (Close of Business)

**Meaning:** The end of the standard business day, after which work matters wait until the next day.

**Synonyms:** *end of business day, day's close, by evening*

**Examples:**
1. The contract must be signed and returned before COB tomorrow.
2. I will send the final draft by COB so the client can review it overnight.

---

#### 3. Acknowledge

**Meaning:** To confirm that you have received something, such as an email or document.

**Synonyms:** *confirm receipt, note, recognize*

**Examples:**
1. Please acknowledge receipt of this email so we know it reached you.
2. The HR team acknowledged my leave application within an hour.

---

#### 4. Hand-holding

**Meaning:** Giving extra guidance and support to someone who is new or unsure of a task.

**Synonyms:** *guidance, mentoring, close support*

**Examples:**
1. The new joiner needed a lot of hand-holding during his first project.
2. Once she is confident with the tool, she won't need this much hand-holding.

---

#### 5. Fresher

**Meaning:** A person who has just graduated and is starting their first job, with no prior work experience.

**Synonyms:** *graduate hire, entry-level employee, new recruit*

**Examples:**
1. We are hiring twenty freshers this year straight out of college.
2. The training program is designed to help freshers adjust to office life.

---

#### 6. Cabin

**Meaning:** A closed private room used by a manager or senior employee in an open office.

**Synonyms:** *private office, chamber, enclosed room*

**Examples:**
1. The manager called me into his cabin to discuss the appraisal.
2. Her cabin is right next to the conference room on the third floor.

---

#### 7. Doubt

**Meaning:** A question or point of confusion about something, used the way "query" or "question" is used elsewhere.

**Synonyms:** *question, query, point of confusion*

**Examples:**
1. I have a small doubt about the reimbursement process, can you clarify?
2. Students in the training session were encouraged to raise their doubts.

---

#### 8. Good-to-have

**Meaning:** A feature or skill that is useful but not essential.

**Synonyms:** *nice extra, optional feature, bonus requirement*

**Examples:**
1. Knowledge of Python is good-to-have for this role but not mandatory.
2. The client marked the extra dashboard as good-to-have, not a must-have.

---

#### 9. Must-have

**Meaning:** Something that is absolutely required and cannot be skipped.

**Synonyms:** *essential, non-negotiable requirement, mandatory item*

**Examples:**
1. A valid work visa is a must-have for this overseas assignment.
2. The client listed data security as a must-have for the new system.

---

#### 10. As Such

**Meaning:** Used to mean "therefore" or "because of this," connecting a reason to its result.

**Synonyms:** *therefore, hence, consequently*

**Examples:**
1. The vendor missed the deadline; as such, the payment will be delayed.
2. There is no update from legal yet, as such the contract remains on hold.

---

#### 11. Herewith

**Meaning:** Along with this message or document, used in formal writing to mean "attached here."

**Synonyms:** *attached, enclosed, along with this*

**Examples:**
1. Please find herewith the signed copy of the agreement.
2. I am sending herewith the list of documents required for onboarding.

---

#### 12. Vide

**Meaning:** By means of, or referring to, a particular document or communication (used in formal Indian office writing).

**Synonyms:** *as per, referring to, by way of*

**Examples:**
1. The transfer was approved vide the circular dated 10th August.
2. This request is being processed vide your email of last week.

---

#### 13. At the Outset

**Meaning:** At the very beginning, before anything else is said or done.

**Synonyms:** *to start with, first of all, initially*

**Examples:**
1. At the outset, I would like to thank everyone for joining this call.
2. At the outset of the project, we listed all the key risks.

---

#### 14. In This Regard

**Meaning:** Concerning this particular matter or topic.

**Synonyms:** *on this matter, regarding this, concerning this point*

**Examples:**
1. We need approval from finance; in this regard, please send the request today.
2. The client raised a concern about pricing, and in this regard we have prepared a response.

---

#### 15. With Reference To

**Meaning:** Referring to a previous letter, email, or conversation, usually to introduce the subject of a new message.

**Synonyms:** *referring to, regarding, in connection with*

**Examples:**
1. With reference to your email dated Monday, please find the requested details.
2. With reference to our telephonic discussion, I am sharing the revised quotation.

---

#### 16. Undersigned

**Meaning:** The person who has signed a letter or document, referring to themselves in formal writing.

**Synonyms:** *signatory, the writer, the person signing*

**Examples:**
1. The undersigned confirms acceptance of the terms mentioned above.
2. Any queries may be directed to the undersigned at the number given below.

---

#### 17. Perusal

**Meaning:** A careful reading or review of a document.

**Synonyms:** *review, examination, careful reading*

**Examples:**
1. The report is enclosed for your kind perusal before tomorrow's meeting.
2. Please go through the policy document for perusal and share your comments.

---

#### 18. Felicitate

**Meaning:** To formally honour or congratulate someone, often at an office event or ceremony.

**Synonyms:** *honour, congratulate, recognize*

**Examples:**
1. The CEO felicitated the top performers at the annual function.
2. Long-serving employees were felicitated with mementos during the celebration.

---

#### 19. Token of Appreciation

**Meaning:** A small gift given to show gratitude for someone's effort or contribution.

**Synonyms:** *small gift, gesture of thanks, memento*

**Examples:**
1. The team received a token of appreciation for completing the project ahead of schedule.
2. As a token of appreciation, the client sent a hamper to the entire team.

---

#### 20. Get-together

**Meaning:** A casual gathering of colleagues, often for a celebration or informal bonding.

**Synonyms:** *informal gathering, office party, casual meetup*

**Examples:**
1. The team is planning a small get-together to celebrate the product launch.
2. We had a get-together at a nearby restaurant after the quarter closed.

---

#### 21. Top Brass

**Meaning:** The most senior leaders or decision-makers in an organization.

**Synonyms:** *senior leadership, top management, higher-ups*

**Examples:**
1. The top brass will review the annual budget next week.
2. Only the top brass was informed about the merger before the announcement.

---

#### 22. Office Politics

**Meaning:** The informal power struggles, favouritism, and rivalries that happen within a workplace.

**Synonyms:** *workplace politics, internal rivalry, power play*

**Examples:**
1. He avoids office politics and focuses only on delivering good work.
2. The delayed promotion was blamed on office politics rather than performance.

---

#### 23. Brownie Points

**Meaning:** Credit or approval earned by doing something to please someone in authority.

**Synonyms:** *extra credit, favour points, approval*

**Examples:**
1. Staying late to help finish the client demo earned her brownie points with the boss.
2. He is trying to score brownie points by volunteering for every extra task.

---

#### 24. Manager

**Meaning:** A person responsible for overseeing a team, project, or department.

**Synonyms:** *supervisor, team head, department head*

**Examples:**
1. My manager approved my leave request within a day.
2. The project manager scheduled a review with all stakeholders.

---

#### 25. Employee

**Meaning:** A person who works for a company in exchange for salary or wages.

**Synonyms:** *staff member, worker, team member*

**Examples:**
1. The company has over five hundred employees across three offices.
2. Every employee must complete the compliance training this month.

---

#### 26. Customer

**Meaning:** A person or organization that buys goods or services from a business.

**Synonyms:** *buyer, client, purchaser*

**Examples:**
1. The customer complained about a delay in delivery last week.
2. Our support team resolved the customer's issue within an hour.

---

#### 27. Salary

**Meaning:** The fixed regular payment an employee receives for their work.

**Synonyms:** *pay, wages, remuneration*

**Examples:**
1. My salary gets credited on the last working day of every month.
2. The new offer includes a ten percent increase over her current salary.

---

#### 28. Hike

**Meaning:** A raise or increase, most commonly used for a salary increase.

**Synonyms:** *raise, increment, pay boost*

**Examples:**
1. He switched companies for a thirty percent hike in salary.
2. The management announced a hike for employees in the sales division.

---

#### 29. Perks

**Meaning:** Additional benefits given to employees besides their regular salary.

**Synonyms:** *benefits, extras, add-on benefits*

**Examples:**
1. Free meals and cab service are some of the perks offered by the company.
2. The offer letter listed health insurance as one of the key perks.

---

#### 30. Bonus

**Meaning:** An extra payment given to an employee, often tied to performance or a festival.

**Synonyms:** *incentive payment, extra pay, reward*

**Examples:**
1. Employees received a festival bonus before the holidays.
2. Her strong sales numbers earned her a year-end bonus.

---

#### 31. Half Day

**Meaning:** Taking leave for only part of the working day instead of the whole day.

**Synonyms:** *partial leave, short leave, half-day off*

**Examples:**
1. I am taking a half day today to visit the doctor in the afternoon.
2. He applied for a half day so he could attend his child's school event.

---

#### 32. Resignation

**Meaning:** A formal statement that an employee is leaving their job.

**Synonyms:** *quitting, stepping down, notice of leaving*

**Examples:**
1. She submitted her resignation after accepting an offer from another company.
2. His resignation letter mentioned his last working day as the thirtieth.

---

#### 33. Relieving Letter

**Meaning:** An official letter given by a company confirming that an employee has completed their notice period and formally left.

**Synonyms:** *release letter, exit letter, discharge letter*

**Examples:**
1. The new employer asked for a copy of my relieving letter from my previous company.
2. HR will issue the relieving letter once the exit formalities are complete.

---

#### 34. Experience Letter

**Meaning:** A document from an employer stating the roles, duration, and details of an employee's tenure at the company.

**Synonyms:** *service certificate, employment letter, tenure certificate*

**Examples:**
1. I need my experience letter to apply for the visa process.
2. The HR portal allows former employees to download their experience letter anytime.

---

#### 35. CTC

**Meaning:** Cost to Company, the total amount a company spends on an employee, including salary and all benefits.

**Synonyms:** *total compensation, cost to company, package*

**Examples:**
1. My CTC includes a base salary, bonus, and insurance benefits.
2. The recruiter shared a CTC breakup before finalizing the offer letter.

---

#### 36. In-hand Salary

**Meaning:** The actual amount an employee receives after all deductions, as opposed to the total CTC.

**Synonyms:** *take-home pay, net salary, actual pay*

**Examples:**
1. After tax deductions, my in-hand salary is lower than the CTC mentioned in the offer.
2. She asked HR to clarify the in-hand salary before accepting the position.

---

#### 37. Deputation

**Meaning:** Being temporarily sent to work at another location, department, or organization while still employed by the original one.

**Synonyms:** *temporary assignment, secondment, temporary posting*

**Examples:**
1. He is on deputation to the client's office for the next six months.
2. Employees on deputation continue to receive their salary from the parent company.

---

#### 38. Gratuity

**Meaning:** A lump sum payment given by an employer to an employee as a token of service after a certain number of years.

**Synonyms:** *retirement benefit, service payment, long-service bonus*

**Examples:**
1. Employees who complete five years become eligible for gratuity.
2. The HR team calculated her final settlement including gratuity and leave encashment.

---

#### 39. Provident Fund (PF)

**Meaning:** A retirement savings scheme where both employee and employer contribute a portion of the salary every month.

**Synonyms:** *retirement fund, PF account, savings scheme*

**Examples:**
1. A fixed percentage of my salary is deducted every month for the Provident Fund.
2. You can withdraw your Provident Fund balance after leaving the organization.

---

#### 40. TDS

**Meaning:** Tax Deducted at Source, the tax that an employer deducts from an employee's salary before payment.

**Synonyms:** *source tax deduction, payroll tax deduction, withheld tax*

**Examples:**
1. The TDS on my salary is reflected in my monthly payslip.
2. HR shared the TDS certificate needed for filing income tax returns.

---

#### 41. Sabbatical

**Meaning:** An extended period of leave from work, often to pursue study, travel, or personal reasons.

**Synonyms:** *extended leave, career break, long leave*

**Examples:**
1. She took a six-month sabbatical to complete her master's degree.
2. The company allows employees to apply for a sabbatical after five years of service.

---

#### 42. Compensatory Off

**Meaning:** A day off given to an employee in return for working on a weekend or holiday.

**Synonyms:** *comp off, time off in lieu, replacement day off*

**Examples:**
1. I worked on Sunday for the launch, so I took a compensatory off on Wednesday.
2. Please apply for your compensatory off before the end of the month.

---

#### 43. WFO (Work From Office)

**Meaning:** Working from the physical office premises rather than remotely.

**Synonyms:** *on-site work, office-based work, in-office work*

**Examples:**
1. The company has mandated three days of WFO every week.
2. New joiners are required to be WFO for the first month of onboarding.

---

#### 44. Correspondence

**Meaning:** The exchange of letters, emails, or written communication between people or organizations.

**Synonyms:** *written communication, letters, exchanges*

**Examples:**
1. All correspondence with the client should be marked to the account manager.
2. Keep a record of your correspondence with the vendor in case of disputes.

---

#### 45. Enclosed

**Meaning:** Included along with a letter or document, used to introduce an attachment in formal writing.

**Synonyms:** *attached, included, herewith*

**Examples:**
1. Please find enclosed a copy of the signed purchase order.
2. The offer letter had the salary structure enclosed as an annexure.

---

#### 46. Aforementioned

**Meaning:** Mentioned or referred to earlier in the same document or conversation.

**Synonyms:** *previously mentioned, above-mentioned, earlier stated*

**Examples:**
1. The aforementioned terms apply to all vendors registered with us.
2. Kindly confirm your acceptance of the aforementioned conditions by Friday.

---

#### 47. Henceforth

**Meaning:** From this point onward, used to indicate a change that will apply going forward.

**Synonyms:** *from now on, going forward, hereafter*

**Examples:**
1. Henceforth, all expense claims must be submitted through the new portal.
2. The office timing will change to nine o'clock henceforth.

---

#### 48. Pursuant To

**Meaning:** In accordance with, or as required by, a rule, agreement, or instruction.

**Synonyms:** *in accordance with, as per, following*

**Examples:**
1. Pursuant to the new policy, all laptops must be returned upon exit.
2. This letter is issued pursuant to the discussion held during the last review.

---

#### 49. In Lieu Of

**Meaning:** Instead of, or as a replacement for, something else.

**Synonyms:** *instead of, in place of, as a substitute for*

**Examples:**
1. He was given a day off in lieu of the extra hours worked over the weekend.
2. The company offered a cash payment in lieu of unused leave.

---

#### 50. Vis-à-vis

**Meaning:** In relation to, or in comparison with, something else.

**Synonyms:** *in relation to, compared to, with respect to*

**Examples:**
1. Our pricing looks competitive vis-à-vis the other bidders.
2. The team discussed the new process vis-à-vis the old workflow.

---

#### 51. Apropos

**Meaning:** With reference to, or fitting in with, the topic being discussed.

**Synonyms:** *regarding, with respect to, in connection with*

**Examples:**
1. Apropos your query, the payment will be released by next week.
2. Apropos the discussion this morning, I have shared the updated numbers.

---

#### 52. Modalities

**Meaning:** The specific details, methods, or procedures needed to carry out a plan or agreement.

**Synonyms:** *details, procedures, particulars*

**Examples:**
1. Let's finalize the modalities of the partnership before signing the contract.
2. The two teams met to work out the modalities of the joint event.

---

#### 53. Superior

**Meaning:** A person of higher rank or authority at the workplace.

**Synonyms:** *senior, boss, higher-up*

**Examples:**
1. She reported the issue directly to her superior instead of waiting.
2. All leave requests must be approved by your immediate superior.

---

#### 54. Subordinate

**Meaning:** A person who works under someone else's supervision or authority.

**Synonyms:** *junior colleague, direct report, team member*

**Examples:**
1. The manager assigned the task to his subordinate for execution.
2. A good leader listens to feedback from subordinates as well as peers.

---

#### 55. Designation

**Meaning:** A person's official job title within an organization.

**Synonyms:** *job title, position title, role title*

**Examples:**
1. Please mention your name, designation, and department in the form.
2. His designation changed from associate to senior associate after the review.

---

#### 56. Nominee

**Meaning:** A person officially named to receive certain benefits, such as insurance or provident fund, on someone's behalf.

**Synonyms:** *beneficiary, designated recipient, named person*

**Examples:**
1. You need to update your nominee details in the insurance policy.
2. She listed her mother as the nominee for her Provident Fund account.

---

#### 57. Grade

**Meaning:** A level or rank assigned to an employee that determines role, pay, and responsibilities.

**Synonyms:** *level, band, rank*

**Examples:**
1. He was promoted to the next grade after two years in his current role.
2. Salary structures differ across each grade in the organization.

---

#### 58. Mediclaim

**Meaning:** A health insurance policy provided by an employer to cover medical expenses.

**Synonyms:** *health insurance, medical cover, health policy*

**Examples:**
1. The company's mediclaim policy covers the employee and their immediate family.
2. She used her mediclaim to cover the hospital bill after her surgery.

---

#### 59. Cab Facility

**Meaning:** Company-arranged transport provided to employees for commuting to and from work.

**Synonyms:** *company transport, pick-up and drop service, office cab*

**Examples:**
1. Employees working the night shift are given a cab facility for safety.
2. The cab facility drops staff near their homes after late meetings.

---

#### 60. Canteen

**Meaning:** The dining area within an office premises where employees eat meals.

**Synonyms:** *cafeteria, dining hall, food court*

**Examples:**
1. We usually catch up over lunch in the office canteen.
2. The canteen offers subsidized meals for all employees.

---

#### 61. Intern

**Meaning:** A student or recent graduate working temporarily at a company to gain practical experience.

**Synonyms:** *trainee, apprentice, student worker*

**Examples:**
1. The marketing team has two interns helping with the social media campaign.
2. Her internship performance led to a full-time offer as an intern turned employee.

---

#### 62. Trainee

**Meaning:** A newly hired employee who is still learning the job through structured training.

**Synonyms:** *apprentice, learner, new recruit*

**Examples:**
1. Trainees spend the first month shadowing senior team members.
2. The trainee batch will complete their certification exam next week.

---

#### 63. Boss

**Meaning:** An informal term for one's direct manager or the head of a team or company.

**Synonyms:** *manager, supervisor, superior*

**Examples:**
1. My boss appreciated the work I put into the client presentation.
2. He asked his boss for permission to leave early today.

---

#### 64. HR

**Meaning:** The Human Resources department, responsible for hiring, employee benefits, and workplace policies.

**Synonyms:** *human resources department, personnel department, people team*

**Examples:**
1. You need to raise a ticket with HR to update your bank details.
2. HR conducted an orientation session for the new batch of hires.

---

#### 65. Admin

**Meaning:** The administration department that handles office facilities, supplies, and general support needs.

**Synonyms:** *administration department, facilities team, support staff*

**Examples:**
1. Raise a request with Admin if your workstation needs repair.
2. Admin arranges the seating and refreshments for all internal events.

---

#### 66. Circular

**Meaning:** An official written notice sent by the company to inform all employees about a policy or update.

**Synonyms:** *notice, memo, announcement*

**Examples:**
1. HR issued a circular about the revised holiday calendar for next year.
2. Please read the latest circular regarding the dress code changes.

---

#### 67. Letterhead

**Meaning:** Official company paper printed with the organization's name, logo, and address, used for formal correspondence.

**Synonyms:** *company stationery, official paper, headed paper*

**Examples:**
1. The offer letter was printed on the company's official letterhead.
2. Please issue the certificate on letterhead with the authorized signatory's stamp.

---

#### 68. Notarized

**Meaning:** Officially certified or verified by a notary as genuine or legally valid.

**Synonyms:** *legally certified, officially attested, notary-verified*

**Examples:**
1. The agreement needs to be notarized before it can be submitted.
2. Please get the affidavit notarized and courier it to our legal team.

---

#### 69. Reimbursement

**Meaning:** Money paid back to an employee for expenses they have already covered out of their own pocket.

**Synonyms:** *expense refund, repayment, cost recovery*

**Examples:**
1. Submit your travel bills for reimbursement within seven days of the trip.
2. The reimbursement for the client dinner was credited to my account this week.

---

#### 70. Petty Cash

**Meaning:** A small amount of cash kept in the office to cover minor day-to-day expenses.

**Synonyms:** *small cash fund, office cash, cash float*

**Examples:**
1. The office boy used petty cash to buy stationery for the meeting.
2. Any petty cash spent must be recorded with a receipt.

---

#### 71. Stationery

**Meaning:** Office supplies such as pens, paper, files, and notepads used in daily work.

**Synonyms:** *office supplies, writing materials, office stock*

**Examples:**
1. Please raise a request with Admin for new stationery for the team.
2. The store room ran out of stationery, so we ordered more folders and pens.

---

#### 72. Xerox

**Meaning:** A commonly used term in India for photocopying a document, originally a printer brand name.

**Synonyms:** *photocopy, duplicate, copy*

**Examples:**
1. Please Xerox these forms and keep one copy for our records.
2. The shop near the office does Xerox and printing for a small fee.

---

#### 73. Courier

**Meaning:** To send a document or package through a delivery service, or the delivery service itself.

**Synonyms:** *send by post, ship, dispatch*

**Examples:**
1. Please courier the signed contract to the head office by tomorrow.
2. The courier arrived with the new laptop for the marketing team.

### Tier 1 — Intermediate

#### 74. Kindly

**Meaning:** A polite way to make a request, often used instead of "please."

**Synonyms:** *please, courteously, graciously*

**Examples:**
1. Kindly send me the updated invoice by tomorrow morning.
2. Kindly confirm your availability for the 3 PM client call.

---

#### 75. Revert

**Meaning:** To reply or respond back to someone, usually in writing.

**Synonyms:** *reply, respond, write back*

**Examples:**
1. Please revert with your feedback on the proposal by Friday.
2. I will revert to you once I have checked with the finance team.

---

#### 76. Please find attached

**Meaning:** A standard phrase used to point to a file attached to an email.

**Synonyms:** *attached herewith, enclosed, attached for reference*

**Examples:**
1. Please find attached the revised budget sheet for Q3.
2. Please find attached the minutes from yesterday's review meeting.

---

#### 77. Regards

**Meaning:** A polite closing used at the end of an email or letter.

**Synonyms:** *best wishes, sincerely, warm regards*

**Examples:**
1. Looking forward to your response. Regards, Priya.
2. Thanks for your time today. Regards, the HR team.

---

#### 78. Update

**Meaning:** New information about the progress of a task or project.

**Synonyms:** *status, progress report, latest information*

**Examples:**
1. Can you give me an update on the client onboarding process?
2. I'll send a status update after the team stand-up.

---

#### 79. Follow up

**Meaning:** To check on something again after some time has passed.

**Synonyms:** *check back, chase, pursue further*

**Examples:**
1. I'll follow up with the vendor if we don't hear back by Wednesday.
2. Please follow up with IT regarding the laptop request.

---

#### 80. At the earliest

**Meaning:** As soon as possible.

**Synonyms:** *ASAP, promptly, without delay*

**Examples:**
1. Kindly share the signed contract at the earliest.
2. We need the report finalized at the earliest to meet the deadline.

---

#### 81. As per

**Meaning:** According to or in line with something already stated.

**Synonyms:** *according to, in accordance with, per*

**Examples:**
1. As per our last discussion, the deployment will happen next Monday.
2. As per company policy, all expenses above ₹5,000 need manager approval.

---

#### 82. Do the needful

**Meaning:** To take whatever action is required to complete a task.

**Synonyms:** *take necessary action, handle it, proceed accordingly*

**Examples:**
1. Kindly do the needful to close this ticket by end of day.
2. Please do the needful and share the access credentials to the new joinee.

---

#### 83. Needful

**Meaning:** Whatever is required or necessary in a given situation.

**Synonyms:** *required action, necessary steps, what is needed*

**Examples:**
1. The finance team will do the needful once the invoice is approved.
2. Please arrange the needful for the client's visit next week.

---

#### 84. Intimate

**Meaning:** To formally inform or notify someone about something.

**Synonyms:** *inform, notify, advise*

**Examples:**
1. Please intimate the team about the change in office timings.
2. I have intimated HR about my leave dates for next month.

---

#### 85. Circulate

**Meaning:** To share a document or information with a group of people.

**Synonyms:** *distribute, share, send around*

**Examples:**
1. Please circulate the agenda before tomorrow's meeting.
2. The HR team will circulate the new dress code policy today.

---

#### 86. Coordinate

**Meaning:** To organize people or tasks so they work together smoothly.

**Synonyms:** *organize, align, arrange*

**Examples:**
1. Can you coordinate with the logistics team for the event setup?
2. I will coordinate between both departments to close this issue.

---

#### 87. Escalate

**Meaning:** To raise an issue to a higher authority when it isn't resolved.

**Synonyms:** *raise, flag, elevate*

**Examples:**
1. If the vendor doesn't respond by Friday, we'll have to escalate this to their manager.
2. Please escalate any critical bugs directly to the project lead.

---

#### 88. Deadline

**Meaning:** The date or time by which something must be completed.

**Synonyms:** *due date, cutoff, target date*

**Examples:**
1. The deadline for submitting the report is this Friday, 6 PM.
2. We are working extra hours to meet the project deadline.

---

#### 89. Action item

**Meaning:** A specific task assigned to someone after a meeting or discussion.

**Synonyms:** *task, to-do, assignment*

**Examples:**
1. The action item from today's call is to share the pricing sheet by tomorrow.
2. Please update the status of all action items before the next review.

---

#### 90. Deliverable

**Meaning:** The final output or result that has to be produced for a project.

**Synonyms:** *output, end product, result*

**Examples:**
1. The first deliverable for this project is the requirement document.
2. All deliverables must be submitted before the client demo on Thursday.

---

#### 91. Confirm

**Meaning:** To make sure something is correct or agreed upon.

**Synonyms:** *verify, validate, ensure*

**Examples:**
