# Business English Vocabulary Book
### 1000+ essential words for Indian and European/American corporate environments

---

## Part 1: Indian Corporate Environment

### Tier 1 — Intermediate

**1. Kindly**
Meaning: A polite way to make a request, often used instead of "please."
Synonyms: please, courteously, graciously
Example 1: Kindly send me the updated invoice by tomorrow morning.
Example 2: Kindly confirm your availability for the 3 PM client call.

**2. Revert**
Meaning: To reply or respond back to someone, usually in writing.
Synonyms: reply, respond, write back
Example 1: Please revert with your feedback on the proposal by Friday.
Example 2: I will revert to you once I have checked with the finance team.

**3. Please find attached**
Meaning: A standard phrase used to point to a file attached to an email.
Synonyms: attached herewith, enclosed, attached for reference
Example 1: Please find attached the revised budget sheet for Q3.
Example 2: Please find attached the minutes from yesterday's review meeting.

**4. Regards**
Meaning: A polite closing used at the end of an email or letter.
Synonyms: best wishes, sincerely, warm regards
Example 1: Looking forward to your response. Regards, Priya.
Example 2: Thanks for your time today. Regards, the HR team.

**5. Update**
Meaning: New information about the progress of a task or project.
Synonyms: status, progress report, latest information
Example 1: Can you give me an update on the client onboarding process?
Example 2: I'll send a status update after the team stand-up.

**6. Follow up**
Meaning: To check on something again after some time has passed.
Synonyms: check back, chase, pursue further
Example 1: I'll follow up with the vendor if we don't hear back by Wednesday.
Example 2: Please follow up with IT regarding the laptop request.

**7. At the earliest**
Meaning: As soon as possible.
Synonyms: ASAP, promptly, without delay
Example 1: Kindly share the signed contract at the earliest.
Example 2: We need the report finalized at the earliest to meet the deadline.

**8. As per**
Meaning: According to or in line with something already stated.
Synonyms: according to, in accordance with, per
Example 1: As per our last discussion, the deployment will happen next Monday.
Example 2: As per company policy, all expenses above ₹5,000 need manager approval.

**9. Do the needful**
Meaning: To take whatever action is required to complete a task.
Synonyms: take necessary action, handle it, proceed accordingly
Example 1: Kindly do the needful to close this ticket by end of day.
Example 2: Please do the needful and share the access credentials to the new joinee.

**10. Needful**
Meaning: Whatever is required or necessary in a given situation.
Synonyms: required action, necessary steps, what is needed
Example 1: The finance team will do the needful once the invoice is approved.
Example 2: Please arrange the needful for the client's visit next week.

**11. Intimate**
Meaning: To formally inform or notify someone about something.
Synonyms: inform, notify, advise
Example 1: Please intimate the team about the change in office timings.
Example 2: I have intimated HR about my leave dates for next month.

**12. Circulate**
Meaning: To share a document or information with a group of people.
Synonyms: distribute, share, send around
Example 1: Please circulate the agenda before tomorrow's meeting.
Example 2: The HR team will circulate the new dress code policy today.

**13. Coordinate**
Meaning: To organize people or tasks so they work together smoothly.
Synonyms: organize, align, arrange
Example 1: Can you coordinate with the logistics team for the event setup?
Example 2: I will coordinate between both departments to close this issue.

**14. Escalate**
Meaning: To raise an issue to a higher authority when it isn't resolved.
Synonyms: raise, flag, elevate
Example 1: If the vendor doesn't respond by Friday, we'll have to escalate this to their manager.
Example 2: Please escalate any critical bugs directly to the project lead.

**15. Deadline**
Meaning: The date or time by which something must be completed.
Synonyms: due date, cutoff, target date
Example 1: The deadline for submitting the report is this Friday, 6 PM.
Example 2: We are working extra hours to meet the project deadline.

**16. Action item**
Meaning: A specific task assigned to someone after a meeting or discussion.
Synonyms: task, to-do, assignment
Example 1: The action item from today's call is to share the pricing sheet by tomorrow.
Example 2: Please update the status of all action items before the next review.

**17. Deliverable**
Meaning: The final output or result that has to be produced for a project.
Synonyms: output, end product, result
Example 1: The first deliverable for this project is the requirement document.
Example 2: All deliverables must be submitted before the client demo on Thursday.

**18. Confirm**
Meaning: To make sure something is correct or agreed upon.
Synonyms: verify, validate, ensure
Example 1: Can you confirm the meeting time before I book the conference room?
Example 2: Please confirm receipt of this email at your earliest convenience.

**19. Schedule**
Meaning: To fix a time for an event or meeting.
Synonyms: plan, arrange, set up
Example 1: Let's schedule a call with the client for tomorrow afternoon.
Example 2: I have scheduled the training session for next Tuesday.

**20. Reschedule**
Meaning: To change a previously fixed time to a new one.
Synonyms: postpone, rearrange, move

Example 2: Could we reschedule our one-on-one to later this week?

**21. Loop in**
Meaning: To include someone in an email thread or discussion.
Synonyms: include, add, bring in
Example 1: Let's loop in the finance team on this thread for visibility.
Example 2: I'll loop in my manager for approval on the budget increase.

**22. CC (carbon copy)**

Synonyms: copy, include as recipient

**23. Touch base**
