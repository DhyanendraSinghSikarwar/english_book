# Corporate Verbs Vocabulary Book

**Common verbs used in workplace English — emails, meetings, reports, negotiations, and appraisals**

Organized in tiers from the most common everyday verbs to advanced leadership and strategy verbs.

---

## Tier 1 — Intermediate

### 1. send

**Meaning:** to make a message, document, or item go from you to someone else

**Synonyms:** *dispatch, transmit, deliver*

**Examples:**
1. Can you send the revised contract to the client before noon?
2. I'll send you the file as soon as the numbers are finalized.

---

### 2. call

**Meaning:** to phone someone or to speak to them by voice

**Synonyms:** *phone, ring, dial*

**Examples:**
1. Please call the vendor to confirm the delivery time.
2. She called me last night to talk about the new role.

---

### 3. meet

**Meaning:** to come together with someone in person or online to talk about something

**Synonyms:** *gather, convene, get together*

**Examples:**
1. Let's meet on Thursday to go through the quarterly numbers.
2. I met the new hire briefly during her first week.

---

### 4. check

**Meaning:** to look at something carefully to make sure it is correct or working

**Synonyms:** *inspect, verify, examine*

**Examples:**
1. Please check the figures before you send them out.
2. I checked the invoice twice and found a small error.

---

### 5. write

**Meaning:** to put words on paper or a screen to communicate something

**Synonyms:** *compose, draft, pen*

**Examples:**
1. I need to write a short summary for the board meeting.
2. He wrote a polite email asking for an extension on the deadline.

---

### 6. ask

**Meaning:** to put a question to someone or to request something from them

**Synonyms:** *inquire, request, query*

**Examples:**
1. She asked her manager for feedback on the presentation.
2. I asked him whether the shipment had arrived yet.

---

### 7. reply

**Meaning:** to answer a message, letter, or question

**Synonyms:** *answer, respond, write back*

**Examples:**
1. Please reply to the client within 24 hours.
2. He replied to my email with the numbers I needed.

---

### 8. respond

**Meaning:** to say or do something in return to a question, message, or situation

**Synonyms:** *reply, react, answer*

**Examples:**
1. The support team responded quickly to the customer's complaint.
2. We need to respond to the vendor's proposal by Friday.

---

### 9. explain

**Meaning:** to make something clear so others can understand it

**Synonyms:** *clarify, describe, spell out*

**Examples:**
1. Can you explain how the new pricing model works?
2. She explained the reasons behind the delay to the whole team.

---

### 10. plan

**Meaning:** to think ahead and decide how something will be done

**Synonyms:** *map out, prepare, design*

**Examples:**
1. We are planning the product launch for early next month.
2. They planned the training sessions around the new hires' schedules.

---

### 11. prepare

**Meaning:** to get something or someone ready for a future event

**Synonyms:** *get ready, arrange, set up*

**Examples:**
1. I'm preparing the slides for tomorrow's client meeting.
2. She prepared a list of questions before the interview.

---

### 12. start

**Meaning:** to begin doing something

**Synonyms:** *begin, kick off, launch*

**Examples:**
1. We will start the project as soon as the budget is approved.
2. He started writing the report right after the call ended.

---

### 13. finish

**Meaning:** to complete something or bring it to an end

**Synonyms:** *complete, wrap up, conclude*

**Examples:**
1. Can you finish the draft by tomorrow morning?
2. The team finished the audit two days ahead of schedule.

---

### 14. decide

**Meaning:** to choose something after thinking about it

**Synonyms:** *determine, resolve, settle on*

**Examples:**
1. The committee decided to postpone the launch by two weeks.
2. We haven't decided which vendor to go with yet.

---

### 15. figure out

**Meaning:** to think about something until you understand it or find a solution

**Synonyms:** *work out, solve, determine*

**Examples:**
1. We need to figure out why the numbers don't match.
2. She figured out a way to fit both meetings into the same afternoon.

---

### 16. sort out

**Meaning:** to deal with a problem or organize something that is messy or unclear

**Synonyms:** *resolve, straighten out, fix*

**Examples:**
1. Can you sort out the billing issue with the client today?
2. We spent the morning sorting out the schedule conflicts.

---

### 17. follow through

**Meaning:** to complete an action or promise you have started

**Synonyms:** *carry through, complete, deliver on*

**Examples:**
1. He said he'd follow through on the client's request by Friday.
2. It's important to follow through on the commitments made in the meeting.

---

### 18. look into

**Meaning:** to examine or investigate something in order to learn more about it

**Synonyms:** *investigate, examine, check into*

**Examples:**
1. I'll look into why the shipment hasn't arrived yet.

---

### 19. set up

**Synonyms:** *arrange, establish, organize*

### 20. come up with

**Synonyms:** *devise, generate, produce*

---

### 21. point out

**Synonyms:** *highlight, note, indicate*

**Examples:**
1. She pointed out a mistake in the second paragraph of the report.
2. He pointed out that the deadline had already passed.

---

### 22. bring up

**Synonyms:** *raise, mention, introduce*

### 23. put off

**Synonyms:** *postpone, delay, defer*

### 24. work out

**Meaning:** to plan or solve something through effort or discussion

### 25. catch up

**Synonyms:** *get updated, keep pace, get current*

### 26. go over

---

### 27. choose

**Meaning:** to pick one option from a number of possibilities

**Synonyms:** *select, pick, opt for*

---

**Synonyms:** *raise, boost, grow*

**Examples:**

1. We changed the meeting time to fit everyone's schedule.
2. The company changed its return policy last month.

---

### 34. develop

**Meaning:** to create or improve something over time, such as a product, plan, or skill

**Synonyms:** *build, create, grow*

1. The team is developing a new app for internal use.

**Synonyms:** *construct, develop, create*

**Examples:**

---

### 36. create

**Meaning:** to make something new that did not exist before

**Synonyms:** *produce, generate, build*

**Examples:**

---

### 37. test

**Meaning:** to try something out to see if it works correctly

**Examples:**
1. We tested the new website before making it public.

**Synonyms:** *repair, correct, resolve*

**Examples:**
1. The IT team fixed the login problem within an hour.

**Synonyms:** *show, display, deliver*

**Examples:**
1. He will present the quarterly results to the board tomorrow.

---

### 42. notify

**Synonyms:** *inform, tell, alert*

**Examples:**
1. We will notify all staff about the schedule change by email.

1. The company will launch the new app early next year.

---

**Synonyms:** *contrast, weigh against, measure against*

1. We compared the two vendors' pricing before making a decision.

1. We need to evaluate the candidates before Friday's decision.

**Synonyms:** *coach, guide, advise*

1. She mentors two junior analysts on the finance team.

## Tier 2 — Upper-Intermediate

### 71. Communicate

**Examples:**
1. Please communicate the new deadline to the whole team before lunch.
2. She communicates project risks to leadership every Monday.

---

### 72. Facilitate

**Examples:**
1. The HR lead will facilitate today's workshop on remote onboarding.
2. Good documentation facilitates a smoother handover between teams.

---

### 73. Highlight

**Meaning:** To draw attention to something important

**Synonyms:** *emphasize, spotlight, underline*

**Examples:**
1. The report highlights three areas where costs can be cut.
2. He highlighted the client's main concern during the debrief.

---

### 74. Outline

### 78. Quantify

### 79. Validate
